Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data GET.ride collects when you use our mobile apps and website (getride.my), why we collect it, who sees it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It applies to passengers, to partners (drivers and delivery partners) and to anyone a booking is made for. It is written with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) in mind.

1. Who we are

GET.ride is run by PUNCAK NIAGA BERSAMA SDN. BHD. (202201025129 (1470826-D)), a company in Malaysia, trading as GET.ride. We decide how and why your personal data is used. For any question or request about your data, email support@getride.my.

2. Your account and signing in

  • Mobile number. Your account is your mobile number. We check it with a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • Profile. Your name, and if you add them, your email, profile photo, gender, date of birth, nationality and address. Your nationality, or else your phone's region, sets the order dates are shown in.
  • PIN. If you set a sign-in PIN, we store only a one-way hash of it. We never store or see the PIN itself.
  • Passkeys. Where passkeys are turned on, you can sign in with your phone's face, fingerprint or screen lock. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your phone: we keep only the passkey's public key and its name.
  • Devices. We keep a record of the phones and browsers signed in to your account (device name, platform, app version and when it signed in), so we can limit how many are signed in at once and sign one out, for example when you sign in on a new phone.
  • Store review demo account. App store reviewers can sign in to one sealed demo account that only runs the app's simulations. Their sign-ins are logged (time, address, device and app version). It never contains real people's data.

3. Identity checks

Partners, and staff of taxi stands and fleets who are asked to verify who they are, give us an identity document (MyKad or passport: the number, the name on it, the expiry date and photos of it) and a live selfie. Partners also give their driving licence, permits, insurance, vehicle documents and vehicle details (plate, make, model). Business accounts give their company's SSM details and document.

Who reads your documents

The admin chooses which of these readers is used, and in what order:

  • Your phone. The app first reads the text on the photo on your own phone (Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone).
  • Our own office reader (NAS). A computer in GET.ride's office reads ID cards, plates and document numbers. It gets a link to the photo that works for 5 minutes, reads it in memory and sends back only what it read (for an ID: name, ID or passport number, date of birth, nationality and expiry).
  • Google (Gemini and Cloud Vision). Partners' document photos are checked by Google Gemini: is it a real document, the one asked for, and what does it say. When an ID card can't be read on the phone or by our office reader, and the admin has it on, the front of the ID is read by Google Cloud Vision. The photo is sent to Google to be read and is not kept by our server function that sends it.
  • Our staff. A person at GET.ride reviews documents and approves or rejects them. Automatic readings only help them.

Face check

When you take a live selfie, the app checks on your phone that a real face is in front of the camera. To help our staff compare your selfie with the photo on your ID (and, for partners going online, with your profile photo), a similarity score is worked out by one of these, in the order the admin sets: our own face-matching server in GET.ride's office, Amazon Web Services (Rekognition), or the admin's own web browser. Only the score is kept with the check. The score never approves or rejects anyone on its own; a person decides.

4. Location

  • While you use the app: to set your pickup point, show cars and prices near you, and share your position with your driver or passenger during a trip.
  • Partners, in the background: while you are online, on a trip or running Meter Digital, so you can be sent requests near you and the trip's route and distance can be recorded for the fare, the receipt and your statements.
  • Partners who allow "Always" / "All the time": your approximate location about every 15 minutes, or when you move about 200 m, even when the app is closed, so our safety and support team can see where you last were.
  • Passengers, in the background: only during your own trip, from pickup to drop-off. The same closed-app check-ins (about every 15 minutes or 200 m) run for a passenger only when all of these are true: GET.ride has turned the feature on, you have turned on "Share my last location with GET.ride safety when the app is closed" in Settings, and your phone allows location "Always". It is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Who sees it: your driver or passenger, only during your trip; people you share a trip link with; Family members as described in section 10; and GET.ride's safety and support team on its live map (God View). God View is open only to staff given access to it, and a sub-admin sees only their own region. Looking at the route someone took, or asking a phone for its live position, is logged with who did it and when. Looking at a Family member this way needs a reason, is logged, and the member can see that log.
  • Location history linked to your account is kept for 90 days, for safety, support and fraud checks.

5. Microphone and audio recordings

Partners can record audio during trips. A driver who turns on VoiceProtection records the sound in the car during their trips, from Start trip to the end, for everyone's safety. The passenger sees a notice on their trip screen while recording is on ("Your driver is recording audio for safety"), and it goes when recording stops or the trip ends.

Driver recording (VoiceProtection)

  • Off unless the driver turns it on. The driver's trip screen shows when it is on, and so does the passenger's: "Your driver is recording audio for safety", with a link to this section. Family members who can see the trip (section 10) see it too.
  • The recording is kept encrypted on the driver's phone, can't be played there, and is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • It is used only for safety and to look into a dispute or a report about that trip. If such a report is made, our support team can ask the phone to upload that recording to a private storage area.
  • Who can listen: only authorised GET.ride staff (our support and safety team and administrators). The app doesn't let the driver or the passenger play it, and it is not used for marketing.

Passenger recording (Audio protection)

  • Your own choice: off unless you turn it on, and only where GET.ride offers it. While your trip is under way, your phone records the sound in the car. Your driver is told that Audio protection is on.
  • The recording stays encrypted on your phone, can't be played there, and is deleted automatically after the time set for your area (24 hours unless your area sets otherwise, never more than 7 days).
  • If a problem with the trip is reported, our support team can ask your phone to upload it to the same private storage area, where only authorised GET.ride staff can listen to it.

Voice notes in chat

You can record a short voice note (up to 5 minutes) in a trip's chat. It is stored privately with the trip. The person you send it to can play it, and authorised GET.ride staff when looking into a report.

Calls

In-app calls with your driver, passenger or support go over the internet (VoIP), directly between the two phones where possible, otherwise through a relay server. On iPhone, incoming calls use Apple's call screen (CallKit) and VoIP push; on Android they can ring over the lock screen. Your phone number is not shown to the other side. Calls are not recorded. We keep a record that a support call happened (who, when and how long).

6. Camera, photos and files

The camera and your photos are opened only when you tap a button. The app sees only the photo or file you pick.

  • Photos of your ID, documents and vehicle, and your live selfie (see section 3).
  • Your profile photo.
  • Photos you send in a trip's chat or to support.
  • Freight and Courier: an optional photo of the cargo when you book, and the driver's proof-of-delivery photos at pickup and drop-off, with the sender's signature if given.
  • Scanning QR codes (payments, wallet, partner codes).

Chat photos, cargo photos and documents are stored in private storage and shown through short-lived links, never at a public address.

7. Bluetooth and nearby devices

  • Pickup and trip check (where GET.ride has turned it on): your phone and your driver's phone check that they are next to each other, to confirm you are getting into the right car and are still together during the trip. Each phone sends a short code that changes every trip and never contains your name or number. It is never used to find where you are. If the network drops, chat messages can go phone to phone.
  • Partners' equipment: a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi OBD-II reader so Meter Digital can read the vehicle's speed and distance, and a receipt printer. Reader and printer settings are kept on your phone.

8. Notifications, SMS and News and offers

  • Service messages always come: trip updates, offers, calls, chat, payments, safety, document and account messages, by push notification, and by SMS where needed (for example sign-in codes, trip links and SOS alerts to the safety team).
  • News and offers is opt-in. Announcements and promotions from GET.ride, by push notification and by SMS, reach you only if you turn on News and offers (Settings > Notifications, or "Send me news and offers" when the app first explains its permissions). It is off unless you turn it on. Our server refuses to queue a promotion SMS to a number whose account hasn't turned it on, and never sends one to a number with no GET.ride account.
  • Stopping: turn News and offers off in Settings at any time, or reply STOP to a promotion SMS (STOP ALL, UNSUBSCRIBE or BERHENTI work too). Service messages still come.

9. Trips, deliveries and other services

  • All trips: pickup and drop-off points, stops, routes, times, fares, offers, ratings and comments, cancellation reasons, tips, waiting time and add-ons, and the chat between passenger and driver.
  • Booking for someone else: the name and phone number of the person you book for, so the driver can reach them.
  • Freight and Courier: what is sent and its declared value, an optional cargo photo, the recipient's name and phone number (sent a tracking link by SMS, and by WhatsApp where GET.ride has set that up), proof-of-delivery photos and signature, and for Courier a confirmation code. A bidding driver doesn't see the recipient's details or the declared value until they take the job.
  • City to City: the trip, seats, the payment method and any posted trips.
  • myGiG (street hail): the driver enters the passenger's phone number. The driver may see whether the number has a GET.ride account, the name, and part of the ID number as GET.ride allows (the last 4 digits by default). The passenger gets the trip and its price in the app, by an SMS link, or by a WhatsApp link sent from the driver's own phone.
  • Business accounts: the company's name, SSM number and document and registered address; members invited by phone, their role, team and spending limit; and the trips booked on the company.
  • Safety: SOS alerts with your exact position, emergency contacts you add (you can pick them from your phone's contacts: the app reads only the contact you pick), trip share links, and safety and incident reports.
  • Support and referrals: messages, photos and voice notes you send to support, and who invited you and whom you invite.
  • Meter Digital (taxi partners): the meter's trips, fares and vehicle readings.

10. Family and children

  • Members. A Family host invites adults by phone number. Nobody becomes a member without accepting, and a member can leave at any time. The host can book and pay for members within limits the host sets, and sees each member's Family trips and what was spent.
  • "Shared with family". A host sees a member's position only as GET.ride's rule for the area allows and the member agrees: never, while on a Family ride, or (only if the member turns it on) at other times too. While anything is shared, the member's app shows "Shared with family" and who can see. Sharing stops at once when the member leaves. Nothing is shared in secret.
  • Children. Children don't have GET.ride accounts or devices of their own. A parent (the Family host) can add a child profile with the child's name, date of birth and optionally a photo, and books the child's trips. A child's trip starts only with a pickup code the host gives the child; where GET.ride requires it, only women drivers take children, and the host is told at pickup and drop-off.
  • GET.ride accounts are not for children. If you believe a child has given us personal data outside Family, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Payments

  • GET.wallet (your in-app balance), GET.credit (partners' credit for fees and commission) and GET.coin (in-app coins): balances and every transaction, including transfers between accounts, top-ups, refunds, commissions and Family or business payments.
  • Cash and in-app payments for each trip, and GET.Plus membership.
  • Partners: earnings, commission, statements, amounts owed, and the bank account details given for payouts.
  • Card and online payments are taken by licensed payment gateways (the gateway's own page shows its name). We receive the result and a reference. We do not receive or store your full card number.

12. Device, connection and safety data

  • Device model and identifier, operating system, app version and push-notification tokens.
  • Your IP address, internet or mobile provider, approximate location worked out from the IP address (by ipwho.is), and network type, recorded for each session to protect accounts and detect fraud.
  • Phone details recorded with each session: battery level, free storage, the app's permission settings, the mobile network and the phone's local network address.
  • The website getride.my uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, and visitor counts on our own server (Umami) for its public pages. They count page views and measure speed without cookies and without identifying you.

13. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and check who you are.
  • To match passengers with partners, run trips and deliveries, work out fares and process payments and commissions.
  • To approve partners and their vehicles, and keep their documents current.
  • To keep passengers and partners safe: SOS, trip sharing, the pickup check, God View, audio recordings and safety reports.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • To send you service messages and, only if you turned on News and offers, announcements and promotions.
  • To give support and settle disputes.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory duties.

14. Who sees it and who we share it with

  • The other side of your trip. Passengers see their partner's name, photo, vehicle and live location. Partners see the passenger's name, pickup and drop-off points and live location during the trip. Phone numbers stay private: calls go through the app.
  • Family, business account admins and people you share a trip with, as described above.
  • GET.ride staff, each only for the pages their role needs.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
    • Vercel: hosting of the website and web app. Cloudflare: network routing for our web addresses and the secure link to our office computers.
    • Maps, place search and routes, as GET.ride sets them: Google Maps Platform, Amazon Location Service, Apple Maps (on iPhone), and OpenStreetMap services: our own map server, or the public OpenStreetMap search and routing servers (Nominatim and OSRM), which your phone may ask directly with the text you search for and the points of your route.
    • Google (Gemini): AI estimates of a trip's route and fare (your pickup, stops and drop-off), and partners' document checks. Google (Cloud Vision and ML Kit): reading ID cards, as in section 3.
    • Amazon Web Services (Rekognition): the selfie and ID face score, when GET.ride uses it.
    • Apple (APNs) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): delivering push notifications and call alerts.
    • ipwho.is: working out an approximate location (city, region, country) and internet provider from your IP address.
    • Malaysian mobile networks: SMS go out from GET.ride's own SMS gateway phones. WhatsApp (Meta): delivery tracking links to Freight and Courier recipients, where GET.ride has set it up.
    • Payment gateways: taking card and online payments.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if GET.ride's business is transferred, under the same protections.

We do not sell your personal data.

15. Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of these providers store or process data outside Malaysia (for example Google's AI and vision services, Amazon Web Services, Apple and Google's push services, and our cloud hosting). Where that happens we use providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA, and send only what the task needs.

16. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep what the app needs to work.
  • Location history: 90 days.
  • Trip audio on the phone: drivers' recordings 24 hours; passengers' the hours set for their area (24 hours unless set otherwise, at most 7 days). A recording uploaded for a report is kept with that report, and is removed when the account that recorded it is wiped.
  • The SMS queue (messages waiting to go out and what was sent): 30 days.
  • Kept with no end date: trip chat messages and the photos and voice notes sent in them; messages, photos, voice notes and call records in support; and sign-in records (the devices, app versions, IP addresses and times you signed in). They are kept securely, seen only by the people in the conversation and authorised staff, and used only for safety, support, disputes and preventing fraud.
  • When you delete your account, it is closed and hidden at once: you are signed out everywhere, nobody can sign in to it or find it by your number, and your number is free for a new account.
  • We then keep the closed account for the time GET.ride sets (365 days), so we can meet tax and financial record-keeping duties, look into safety reports and fraud, and settle disputes. After that, your personal details are wiped: your name, email, ID number, address, date of birth, gender and nationality; partner documents and their files; emergency contacts, saved places, favourite drivers, personal QR codes and notifications; location history and audio recordings; and your name and number on past trips and safety reports, which then read "Deleted user".
  • Kept with no end date, without your name and phone number: trip and payment records (times, places and fares), the GET.wallet, GET.credit and GET.coin ledger and any amount a partner still owes, ratings and support conversations, and a record that the account was deleted and when.
  • Support records and backups may still hold copies. They are kept securely with no end date and are not used for anything else.

17. Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings > Delete account.
  • On the web: getride.my/delete-account. Sign in with a code sent to your number and delete it straight away, or, if you can't sign in, ask by phone: we text a code to your number, and once you enter it your account is signed out everywhere and deleted after 7 days. Signing in again before then cancels the request.
  • Before you delete, use or settle any GET.wallet, GET.credit or GET.coin balance: balances are frozen when the account is deleted.

18. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: see and edit your profile in the app, or ask us for a copy of your personal data or to correct it.
  • Withdrawing consent at any time, in the app:
    • News and offers (push and SMS): Settings > Notifications, or reply STOP to a promotion SMS.
    • Closed-app location check-ins for passengers: Settings, or change your phone's location setting from "Always".
    • Audio protection (passengers) and VoiceProtection (drivers): turn it off in the app.
    • Location, notifications, camera, microphone and Bluetooth: your phone's settings. Some features, such as booking or driving, need location to work.
  • Limiting processing or deleting your data: ask us, or delete your account as in section 17, subject to what we must keep by law.

To make a request, email support@getride.my. We will answer within the time the PDPA requires.

19. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules let each person read only their own data, and each staff member only what their role needs. PINs are stored only as hashes. Documents, chat media, cargo photos and audio recordings are kept in private storage. Trip audio on a phone is encrypted with a key kept in the phone's secure storage. Wallet balances change only through checked server-side operations. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your PIN and phone safe.
