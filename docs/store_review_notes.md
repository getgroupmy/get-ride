# Store review notes

Paste the text below into App Store Connect (App Review Information > Notes)
and Play Console (App content > App access > instructions). Before a review,
turn on Admin > Settings > App Settings > Store review login (number
60608080); turn it off when the review is done.

---

Sign in: on the first screen enter the phone number +60 608080 (country
Malaysia, then 608080) and tap Next. No SMS code and no PIN are needed for this
number. Several test devices can be signed in at once.

What you will see: a notice listing every permission the app asks for and why
(tap a row for the full explanation), then a choice: "Use as a passenger" or
"Use as a driver". An optional short tour is offered ("Show me around"); skip
it any time, and restart it or switch mode from the side menu > How it works.

This is a sealed demo account. Every feature of both modes works as for a
real user, on simulated data kept on the test phone: demo drivers send offers
and drive your trip, demo requests arrive for you as a driver, the other side
answers chats and calls, a demo wallet balance goes up and down, and demo
payments succeed without real money. A "Demo · simulated" label is always
shown and simulated pages carry a "DEMO" mark. The account cannot reach real
people, real drivers or riders, real payments, chats, calls, SOS or SMS,
cannot become a real driver, and has no admin access; the server enforces
this. Staff with an admin role see an admin area that is not available to
this account.

Every feature, and how to try it (also listed in the app: side menu > How
it works > Try every feature, each with an Open button and, for the booking
services, a "Quick demo trip"):

As a passenger
- Ride now: choose where to, pick a car, tap Find driver. Demo drivers send
  offers; accept one, the car drives to you and on to the drop-off; then pay
  (demo GET.wallet or cash), rate and get the receipt.
- Scheduled ride: on the booking panel pick a later time and book. It is
  listed under Trips > Scheduled; tap "Run it now (demo)" to see it play out,
  or Cancel.
- Hire by time (hourly, daily, weekly, monthly): Hire a driver on the home
  page. Now: demo drivers answer as for a ride. Later or for several days:
  each day is listed under Trips > Scheduled.
- City to City: a whole car to another city, with your fare; demo drivers
  send offers. A seat on a posted trip: search, two demo trips are found,
  book a seat and the trip plays out.
- Courier and Freight: send a parcel or move goods with your offer; demo
  couriers and drivers send offers and deliver. Where the region has them
  switched off, use Try every feature > Quick demo trip.
- Family: you host a demo family (a spouse and a child). Change a limit, add
  a child or member, see the member's demo trip, and book a ride for a member
  ("Who is riding" on the booking panel).
- Chat and call: on a demo trip tap Chat (the demo driver answers) or Call (a
  demo call rings and connects; no real call, the microphone is not used).
- SOS: the red SOS button on a demo trip, or Safety > Emergency SOS. Every
  step is shown; in the demo nothing is sent and no one is alerted, called
  or texted.
- Help: Chat with us opens a demo support chat that answers; its call
  button rings a demo agent.
- GET.wallet: top up the demo wallet, pay rides from it, and pay a shop by QR
  (Try every feature > GET.wallet > Pay by QR; no camera needed).
- My QR: your own codes.

As a driver
- Ride requests: go online; demo requests arrive. Accept, the car drives
  itself, start and complete the trip; the fare goes to the demo GET.credit.
- Offer a fare: on a demo request tap Offer a fare; the demo passenger takes
  it (up to 30% over the asked fare) or declines.
- Scheduled jobs (Drive > Scheduled): accept a demo booking for later, or
  give it back.
- myGiG: enter where the passenger is going and send; the demo passenger
  accepts and the trip plays out.
- GET.credit: Wallet > Recharge GET.credit moves demo GET.wallet money into
  it.
- Earnings, statements, documents and permits, preferences: as for a real
  driver (empty lists for the demo account).

Not simulated (the demo account is told so, and nothing happens): buying,
selling or sending GET.coin, GET.Plus membership, withdrawals, emergency
contacts, profile and phone changes, document uploads, and becoming a real
driver.

GET.ride is a licensed e-hailing operator in Malaysia (APAD intermediation
business licence, LPP). The licence document is available on request.

Permissions, and why:
- Location while using the app: set your pickup, show cars and prices near
  you, and share your position with your driver or passenger during a trip.
- Location all the time: trip tracking, safety and ride requests even when
  the app is closed or not in use: during a trip (passengers and drivers),
  while a driver is online, while Meter Digital runs, and approximate
  check-ins for drivers who allow it. Passengers only during their own trip,
  unless they turn on "Share my last location with GET.ride safety" in
  Settings (off by default). A full explanation is shown before the system
  asks, with Continue and Not now.
- Notifications: driver offers, trip updates, ride requests for drivers,
  chat messages, calls, payments, safety. News and offers only if the user
  ticks "Send me news and offers" (off by default).
- Camera: documents, the live selfie check, parcel photos, chat photos, QR
  codes.
- Microphone: in-app calls and voice notes, and optional Audio protection
  that records trip audio on the phone, encrypted, deleted after 24 hours
  unless a reported problem needs it.
- Bluetooth (nearby devices): confirms the passenger and the car are together
  at pickup (a one-time code, no name or number), and drivers' OBD-II reader
  and receipt printer. Never used to find where you are.
- Calls in the background (Android): keeps an in-app call connected and rings
  incoming calls like a phone call.
- iPhone background modes: voip (an incoming ride call arrives as a PushKit
  VoIP push and is reported to CallKit at once), audio (a call, and Audio
  protection if turned on, keep their audio
  going with the screen off), location (trip tracking, see above),
  remote-notification (notifications).
- Photos (iPhone): attach a photo you pick; only that photo is seen.
- Local network (iPhone, drivers): a Wi-Fi OBD-II reader or receipt printer.
